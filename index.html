📍 National Laboratory Partnership ⏱️ 2022-2024 🎯 Catalysis & Emissions Control

Cu-SSZ-39 Zeolite Catalysts for NOx Reduction

Development of advanced zeolite-based catalysts for automotive emissions control, achieving superior NOx reduction efficiency and thermal stability.

The Challenge

Automotive manufacturers needed catalysts capable of meeting increasingly stringent emissions standards while maintaining performance under harsh operating conditions (high temperatures, thermal cycling, and chemical poisoning). Existing catalysts degraded rapidly, leading to compliance failures and increased costs.

My Approach

Designed and synthesized novel Cu-exchanged SSZ-39 zeolite catalysts using advanced characterization techniques (synchrotron XRD, in-situ spectroscopy) to understand structure-activity relationships. Optimized synthesis parameters through systematic DOE (Design of Experiments) methodology. Conducted pilot-scale testing to validate commercial viability.
